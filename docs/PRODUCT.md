# BOX2 — Product and Stage Operations

Status: **approved direction, not a deployed or operational claim**. Source: owner discussions Oct. 8, 2026. Claims requiring public venue confirmation remain launch gates.

## The promise

**BOX2 — Come Tell It Here First.**
**Everybody starts somewhere. Start here.**
**Bring your joke. Everything else is here.**

BOX2 is a deliberately welcoming *comedy development room*, not an elimination contest or venue wars. Provide readily available performance repetitions with stage, lights, cameras, audio, projector/teleprompter cues, livestream audience, and optional shareable clips. Open to first-timers, working comics, touring comics, people practicing hosting, sketch-comedy creators, and original joke/tag writers. Give the performer focus and creative choice. No pressure to mingle or linger.

Owner personally expects to perform **at least twice per BOX2 night**, unless visiting/supporting another venue. Host shifts can be practiced by other comedians. Live public audiences at other local venues remain part of the owner's skill development. Avoid marketing language insulting those venues.

## Hours and booking

- **Sunday, Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, Saturday:** 9:00 PM–2:00 AM local, end time is **next calendar day**.
- **Tuesday:** no regular BOX2 show; outside live performance (often Akron).
- One Wednesday and one Friday per month may have outside engagements; dashboard must support **date-specific cancellations, adjusted hours, or designated guest hosts**, shown accurately to bookers.
- Stage runs **10-minute allocation blocks**; default performance is **5 minutes** plus transitions, host riff, and buffer. 9 PM–2 AM = 30 blocks. Start with at most **22 online-bookable** and reserve remaining blocks for host discretion, walk-ins, overruns, and second attempts. Admin can configure which blocks are public.
- **Owner-approved arrival policy (2026-10-09):** Arrive **10–20 minutes before** scheduled stage time (not earlier than T−20); **check in immediately upon arrival** and be **on deck by T−10**. A performer arriving at T−10 must check in and be ready at once; this is not a guaranteed late-arrival grace period. Publish the same window in onboarding, booking confirmations, arrival directions, reminder emails and host lineup. An after-midnight set belongs to the previous evening's show date.
- Event may be lightly attended or have quiet gaps. **Do not promise a full house, guaranteed laughs, total views, guaranteed performance, or celebrity visits**.
- Clear state: available / booked / confirmed / checked-in / performed / cancelled / no-show / held. Avoid double booking. Provide waitlist later only if needed.

## Live room culture (use readable user-facing language)

- **Sober stage:** no alcohol/drugs at BOX2; avoid false safety assurances.
- **Comedy only:** no general music performances or spoken word; *musical comedy* is allowed with an instrument a performer can carry in and out by themselves.
- This is near **homes with children**. Arrive and depart respectfully; keep outside noise and parking appropriate. In-person attendees wait in the lobby until called; **get in, get your set, get out**, no pressure to remain or socialize. Online is the social space.
- No talking in the back during sets, disruptive phone use in the performance room, or unauthorized filming. The livestream provides online access on people's phones; those in the room give the comedian their attention.
- Be encouraging. A voluntary fist-bump as someone leaves the stage is welcome, never required (especially for hygiene).
- Dogs may bark; outside noise, food/dinner activity, or some distraction may happen. Practice returning attention to the joke without blaming an audience. Do not promise complete silence.
- Clean shared microphones/contact surfaces **between performers**, per manufacturer-compatible procedures. Disinfect high-touch **lobby and stage-left bathroom surfaces hourly**, with a record/checklist; honor actual disinfectant contact times. Provide hand hygiene; performers may bring compatible personal mics and may practice hand-held microphone technique. **Do not label the site "germ-safe," sterile, or infection-free**. Ask people feeling ill to stay home and cancel/reschedule.
- Comedians may test brand-new material. No obligation that performers must be objectively funny; no public ranking, insult or shame for a rough first attempt.
- Comments/tags may be submitted online, but only displayed/performed when the comic opts in and host moderates; **no uncontrolled heckling of non-consenting performers**. You can deliberately hold a separately labelled opt-in roast/heckle lab later.

## Recording, consent and performers' rights

Separate consent states; do not merely infer consent from booking:
- **A — livestream + recording + approved short clips**: permit live broadcast, BOX2 VOD retention for disclosed window, and clips within the agreed scope. A host must still mark clip candidates and verify restrictions before publication; writers/third-party music need their own rights.
- **B — livestream only, no BOX2 highlight clips**: live broadcast as agreed; document VOD policy precisely and do not repurpose beyond it. Note viewers may record independently.
- **C — private rehearsal, no livestream / no recording**: must be scheduled in an actually private block with streaming and recording stopped; cannot occur on an active public feed. Reserve separate off-stream slots (often later in night or by request).
Never imply a streamed performance is private. For main recorded/feature blocks, clip eligibility can be a published scheduling condition **only when equivalent alternatives remain available and are clearly identified**.
A performer controls BOX2 publication of their material consistent with consent/accepted recordings. A VOD disappearing from Twitch or BOX2 does **not** guarantee third-party copies cease to exist.

## Originality and performance receipts

BOX2 encourages performers to **tell their original jokes for the first time on this stage** if they choose. The working principle is "prove you told it *here* at a recorded time", not prove you were the first person anywhere to write/tell it. A future receipt may include performer name, set/time in `America/New_York` + UTC, show date, reference, authorized recording ID, and content digest. Backdating resistance/independent timestamping are later milestones. Receipts prove at most documented performance at a particular time, **not** copyright registration, provenance of creation, or definitive first authorship. Private unrecorded sets cannot get video-backed proof.

## Social and audience strategy

- **Facebook**: organic promotion to comedians, native events, fast page sharing, public event interest and ads insights; click-to-book attribution using campaign `utm_*`; install Meta Pixel or later server events only behind legitimate consent and disclosures. Do not send sensitive personal data, joke text, or booking emails to analytics.
- **Reddit**: share link only; optionally invite writers organically, but no API integration, scraped comments or bot harassment. Creative "trolls"/heckles only through explicitly consented, moderated feedback.
- **Twitch**: embedded livestream with optional chat. Twitch view count differs from in-room people and may not represent unique people; no invented metrics.
- **TikTok**: share clips only with correct rights/consent and credit. Ownership of a performer's own recordings and joke text remains with relevant creators under terms.
- **Measurement**: Facebook reach/video views/event response → BOX2 landing visits → calendar views → booking attempts → completed bookings → check-ins → performed sets → repeat performances → authorized shared clips → referred bookings. Admin metrics must disclose what is observed vs inferred; don't confuse ad reach with unique conversions.

Flag candidate means editorial review, not implicit publication permission.
- Manage exceptions, open/hold slots, booking cancellation, check-in; import walk-ins with permission.
- Upload or replace parking/walkthrough video and publish associated arrival text.
- View private joke writer submissions with granted permission scopes.
- Sanitation log (between mics; hourly lobby/bathroom), simple host handoff, and recording-mode safety gate.
- Review at most needed performer data; do not expose email, phone, legal name or scripts on livestream/projector.

**Laundry Set**: special featured longer performance "long enough to wash and dry a load". A fun future award, not a guarantee or default 10-minute slot; host manually authorizes and schedules extended sets.
